What Should You Wear?

Discover costume ideas that are creepy, creative, funny, and fun to wear.

01

Classic and Spooky

You can never go wrong with a vampire, witch, ghost, skeleton, or black cat. These timeless looks are easy to customize with makeup, hats, capes, face paint, or accessories. Choose one recognizable detail, like a vampire's cape or a witch's hat, to complete your look without needing an expensive outfit.

02

Funny, Creative, or Pop Culture

If scary costumes are not your style, try a funny food costume, a pun, a favorite fictional character, or a group costume with friends. You could coordinate colors and accessories so everyone belongs to the same theme. Choose an idea that matches your personality and lets your creativity shine.

03

Comfort Comes First

A great costume should let you walk, see, breathe, and move comfortably. Check the weather, wear shoes you can safely walk in, and make sure masks do not block your vision. Reflective details or a small light can help you stay visible outdoors. Reusing clothes you already own can also help keep your costume affordable.
