The History of Halloween

Discover how an ancient festival became one of the world's most recognizable celebrations.

01

From the Festival of Samhain

Halloween has roots in Samhain, an ancient Celtic festival celebrated around the end of October. It marked the end of the harvest and the beginning of winter. Some people believed that the boundary between the living and the dead became thinner at this time of year.

02

Traditions Through the Centuries

Over time, Christian observances such as All Saints' Day blended with older seasonal customs in parts of Europe. The evening before All Saints' Day became known as All Hallows' Eve, which eventually shortened to Halloween. Traditions involving candles, disguises, and gatherings changed from place to place.

03

How Halloween Became Modern

Immigrants brought Halloween customs to North America, where traditions such as parties, pumpkin carving, and trick-or-treating developed. During the twentieth century, Halloween increasingly became a family and neighborhood celebration. Today, people celebrate with costumes, decorations, candy, and spooky stories.
