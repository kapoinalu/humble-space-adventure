Halloween Folklore

Explore eerie legends, old superstitions, and mysterious Halloween stories.

01

Jack-o'-Lanterns and Stingy Jack

An Irish folktale tells of a man called Stingy Jack who tricked the Devil and was left wandering with a glowing ember inside a carved turnip. The story is one traditional explanation for the jack-o'-lantern. Irish immigrants brought lantern-carving customs to North America, where pumpkins became a popular choice because they were large and easy to carve.

02

Spirits and the Thin Boundary

Some traditional beliefs connected with Samhain and Halloween describe this time of year as a period when spirits and the living were closer together. Different communities used fires, disguises, prayers, or other customs to mark the season or protect themselves. These stories reflect traditional beliefs about the supernatural rather than proof that ghosts appear on Halloween.

03

Black Cats, Witches, and Omens

Black cats became linked with Halloween imagery through European folklore that associated cats with witches and supernatural powers. Witches, bats, ravens, and other night creatures also became symbols of mystery and fear in popular stories. Today, these images are commonly used in Halloween decorations, costumes, and spooky entertainment.
